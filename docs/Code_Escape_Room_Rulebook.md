# Code Escape Room Rulebook

**Venue:** TCL 3/4, 3rd floor, right side  
**Time:** 8:00 am - 10:00 am

Please arrive before 8:00 am so that everyone can settle in and we can start on time.

## How the event works

- The event has five rounds of increasing difficulty and a shared two-hour deadline. Everyone's event timer starts together; teams move forward when they solve or skip a round.
- All three registered team members may sign in with their registered Google accounts. Your team shares one question set, progress and score. Coordinate submissions with your teammates.
- The base time limits for rounds 1 to 5 are 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 minutes. Solve within your allowance to earn the full round reward. Unused time carries into the next round.
- After the allowance expires, choose **Continue** for a reduced reward or **Skip** to move forward. The available reward decreases by 5% of the base reward per minute, down to a 25% minimum. Skipping deducts 20% of that round's base reward; your total may go below zero. Overtime and skips carry no unused time.
- Timers keep running while you browse. Submissions close when the event ends. Rankings use total points; ties use rounds solved, then the time that score was reached.

## What is allowed

- Discuss and solve questions with your own teammates.
- Use the internet, search engines, online tools and AI assistants.
- Ask an organizer for help with a login, timer or platform issue.

## Conduct and disqualification

- Do not disturb others, create a nuisance or behave badly toward participants or organizers.
- Do not peep at another team's screen or solutions. Do not share or copy answers across teams.
- Teams found violating these conduct rules will be **disqualified. No refund will be provided.**
